Privacy notice.

What happens to the information you provide through this portfolio.

Site delivery

This portfolio does not use advertising cookies or behavioural analytics. Standard technical request information may be processed by GitHub Pages or another configured hosting provider when the site is delivered.

Visual assets

The site requests its display fonts from Google Fonts and selected project photography from Unsplash. Those providers may receive standard request details such as your IP address, browser information, and the referring page when these assets load.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address, and message are transmitted to FormSubmit so the message can be delivered to Maurice. Do not include passwords, financial details, health information, or other sensitive personal data.

Why information is used

Contact details are used only to read your enquiry, reply to you, and retain necessary professional correspondence. They are not sold or used for advertising.

Your choices

You can contact Maurice directly by email instead of using the form. To request correction or deletion of correspondence, email okurutmauriceleonard@gmail.com.

Third parties

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages, with Vercel configuration also included in the repository. The optional contact form uses FormSubmit. Google Fonts and Unsplash deliver the external visual assets described above. Each provider's own privacy and retention practices may apply.
