Analytics and reporting that answer a clear question.

Transform validated data into useful measures, dashboards, reports, and explanations for the people making decisions.

What this covers

Connect the data to the decision it supports.

Useful when the data exists but the measures are inconsistent and the dashboards go unread. You get agreed definitions, reporting people trust, and an explanation of what the result means for the decision in front of them.

Typical problems addressed

  • Teams produce reports, but key measures are defined differently each time.
  • Decision-makers receive tables without the context needed to act.
  • Dashboard data requires repeated manual preparation before every update.
  • Important quality exceptions are hidden inside averages or totals.

Typical deliverables

Reporting that remains explainable.

The right output may be a dashboard, analytical report, recurring reporting pack, or a combination.

Measure definitions

Clear descriptions of each metric, its source, calculation, frequency, and intended interpretation.

Power BI dashboards

Focused views that help users monitor patterns, compare results, and investigate relevant changes.

Python analysis

Reproducible notebooks or scripts for exploration, transformation, visualisation, and reporting.

Reporting workflow

A documented path from refreshed source data to validated numbers and distributed output.

Exception visibility

Quality notes and operational flags that help users interpret unusual or incomplete results.

Decision narrative

Concise explanations of what the data shows, why it matters, and what requires attention.

Workflow

Design from the audience backwards.

Start with the user and decision, then choose measures and visuals.

Frame

Define the decision, audience, reporting frequency, and questions the output must answer.

Validate

Confirm source quality, calculation rules, and any caveats before presenting results.

Communicate

Build the dashboard or report with a clear hierarchy and concise supporting explanation.

Review

Test interpretation with users and refine measures or views that are unclear or unused.
