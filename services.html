Where this fits

Useful when information exists, but confidence or clarity does not.

These are representative situations rather than fixed packages.

SME operations

Multiple files or systems, manual reporting, inconsistent fields, and a need for repeatable processes.

Programme & field data

Structured collection, validation, record digitisation, progress tracking, and decision-ready activity reporting.

Data product preparation

Cleaning, organising, documenting, and checking datasets before analytics, automation, or modelling.

Engagement structure

A process that keeps the decision in view.

Good data delivery begins with the business question, not the dashboard or model.

Discover

Define users, decisions, source systems, constraints, and the expected output.

Audit

Profile the available data, surface gaps, and agree on quality and reporting rules.

Deliver

Build in reviewable stages with validation, documentation, and clear ownership.

Improve

Monitor recurring runs, refine reporting, and automate checks where they add value.
