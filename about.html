Who I am

An engineer who works across the whole stack.

I am Okurut Maurice Leonard, a solutions architect and full-stack engineer based in Kampala, Uganda. I work across the whole delivery: architecture and data modelling, React interfaces, Python and Node services, SQL systems, reporting, machine learning, and workflow automation.

At Logos Cloud Services, I support data work for SME client contexts spanning fintech, agribusiness, and logistics: automated pipelines, reporting workflows, model-ready datasets, and business information turned into accessible insight. Alongside that I collaborated on Aristoc Booklex, a live e-commerce platform for an established Ugandan bookstore chain, covering catalogue, accounts, cart, and checkout.

My earlier local-government experience strengthened a complementary discipline: handling official records carefully, checking integrity before reporting, documenting procedures, and explaining technical findings in plain language.

What guides the work

  • Reliability before presentation. A polished dashboard cannot compensate for incomplete or inconsistent source data.
  • Context before tooling. The reporting question and operating process determine the right technical approach.
  • Clarity for the end user. Documentation and data storytelling are part of the solution, not an afterthought.
  • Maintainability after delivery. Repeatable workflows, checks, and handover notes make the work usable beyond a single analysis.

Working approach

A simple structure for dependable delivery.

The exact technology changes with the problem. The underlying sequence stays consistent.

Understand

Clarify the decision, user, source systems, constraints, and definition of a useful result.

Structure

Map fields, define consistent formats, and establish validation rules before analysis.

Build

Implement the pipeline, database, report, dashboard, or automation in testable steps.

Handover

Document assumptions, checks, and recurring tasks so the solution remains understandable.
